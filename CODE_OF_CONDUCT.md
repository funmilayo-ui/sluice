# Code of conduct

Sluice is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, disagree about code, and learn without being treated badly.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique the code or the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and kind when reviewing. Say what needs to change and why.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone started somewhere, and "how do I run this?" is a fine question.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and merging, and that they will explain the reason.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests, including spam and off-topic promotion.
- Pressure to merge, or to pay out or credit work, that bypasses the normal review process.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainers through the repository. Reports are handled privately where possible.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which you can read in full for more detail.
